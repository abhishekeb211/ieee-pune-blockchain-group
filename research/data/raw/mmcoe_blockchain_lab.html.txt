Blockchain Server Room-Lab
The Blockchain Server Room-Lab at MMCOE is a cutting-edge facility dedicated to the research, development, and practical application of blockchain technology. Designed to provide students and faculty with the resources needed to explore the evolving world of decentralized systems, this lab is a crucial hub for innovation in the field of blockchain and distributed ledger technologies.
Key Features:
Server With RIG : 19 GPU HPC Server, Processor: 3U 64 Cores Storage: 1TB SSD, M/B: Asus B250ME. GPU: Support upto 19, GUP Sets 8 GB Each, Power Supply :-100-240VAC 50/60HZ, RAM: 32GB. DDR4 (2*16GB), GPU Mining RIG For BTC & ETH, Mining,Blockchain Mining Chipsets,Blockchain GPU Accelerator & Compute,KR-PDU-50KVA Power Supply, Unit 42 U Server Rack.
State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: The lab is equipped with high-performance servers and specialized hardware optimized for running blockchain nodes, smart contract development, and decentralized applications (dApps).
Blockchain Frameworks & Tools: Students and researchers have access to various blockchain platforms such as Ethereum, Hyperledger, Solana, and others, along with the latest development tools like Truffle Suite, Ganache, Solidity, and web3.js.
Security & Cryptography: With a focus on security, the lab offers an environment for studying cryptographic techniques, consensus algorithms (e.g., Proof of Work, Proof of Stake), and best practices in securing blockchain networks.
Real-Time Network Simulations: The lab supports the creation of private blockchain networks for testing and simulating real-world scenarios, allowing for hands-on experience in blockchain deployment and management.
Collaborative Environment: A place for interdisciplinary collaboration, the lab encourages students and researchers from computer science, finance, law, and economics to work together on blockchain-related projects.
Purpose:
The Blockchain Server Room-Lab is an integral part of MMCOE’s vision to foster cutting-edge technological education and research. The lab provides students with the tools and environment needed to:
Develop and deploy blockchain applications.
Study and implement smart contracts and tokenomics.
Gain practical knowledge of blockchain protocols and consensus mechanisms.
Contribute to innovative research in the fields of digital currencies, decentralized finance (DeFi), and supply chain management.
Learning & Research Opportunities
Academic Courses: Hands-on learning modules that cover blockchain architecture, cryptography, decentralized finance, and the future of distributed technologies.
Workshops & Hackathons: Regular workshops and hackathons aimed at encouraging creative solutions for real-world problems using blockchain technology.
Industry Collaborations: The lab serves as a bridge between academia and industry, offering students opportunities for internships, live projects, and collaborations with blockchain startups and leading companies.
Technologies Used
Blockchain Platforms: Ethereum, Hyperledger, Solana
Smart Contract Languages: Solidity, Rust
Development Frameworks: Truffle Suite, Hardhat
Cryptography: SHA-256, Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC)
Consensus Mechanisms: Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS)
Decentralized Finance (DeFi): Uniswap, Aave, MakerDAO
Containerization: Docker, Kubernetes
Cloud Platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure

About the Group

Advancing blockchain in Pune, together

The IEEE Pune Blockchain Group is a local chapter aligned with the mission of the IEEE Blockchain Technical Community: to promote educational and outreach activities and collaborate with organizations working in the blockchain space — forming a vibrant network of professionals, researchers, and students interested in networking, collaborating, learning, sharing, and advancing technology across Maharashtra, India, and the broader Region 10 (APAC).

🎓

Students

University blockchain clubs and student branch members across Pune get access to hackathons, mentorship, and entrepreneurship programs run in partnership with the global IEEE Blockchain Technical Community.

🔬

Researchers

A forum to present work, exchange ideas, and collaborate on distributed ledger technology, cryptography, consensus mechanisms, and Web3 research with peers across Region 10 (APAC) and beyond.

🏛️

Academicians

Channels for curriculum development, joint publications, faculty development programs, and outreach activities that bring blockchain education into classrooms and premier institutions across Pune.

💼

Industry Professionals

Networking with practitioners building real-world enterprise blockchain, FinTech, and DLT applications in Pune's IT and tech corridors, plus discounted access to IEEE educational content and conferences.

Past Events

From Pune to the flagship global stage

Since 2023, the community has helped organize and support marquee IEEE blockchain gatherings — from the India Blockchain Forum editions to the flagship international conference co-hosted in Pune.

1st IEEE India Blockchain Forum

September 8–9, 2023

Satish Dhawan Auditorium, IISc Bangalore

The inaugural IEEE India Blockchain Forum brought together blockchain researchers, industry experts, and the startup ecosystem to explore advancements and opportunities in blockchain technology.
